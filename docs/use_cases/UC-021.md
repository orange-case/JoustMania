# Use Case: Play Team Joust

## Overview

**Use Case ID:** UC-021
**Use Case Name:** Play Team Joust (Teams, Random Teams)
**Primary Actor:** Player
**Goal:** Be on the last team with players alive
**Status:** Tested
**Requirements:** FR-021, FR-027, FR-030, FR-033

The game lifecycle is the base of [UC-020](UC-020.md) (BR-005 to BR-009 there); this use case states only the Team Joust delta (BR-004) and its own flows.

## Preconditions

- A JoustTeams or JoustRandomTeams game was started with at least three players, the mode's minimum (UC-010 BR-001). `num_teams` (2 to 6, default 2) comes from `game.json`.

## Main Success Scenario

1. System sorts players by serial, shuffles them if `random_assignment` is on, and assigns teams round-robin.
2. System colours each player with the team colour and runs the team countdown (team colour, white, green).
3. Players play as in UC-020 steps 3 to 5; deaths are detected as in [UC-030](UC-030.md) (Teams uses the default kill gate).
4. When only one team has players alive, System publishes `team_winner` (team, name, colour, winners), shows the winners a rainbow and announces "<colour> team wins" (Blue, Magenta, Green, Yellow and Turquoise have a voice line; Pink, Orange and Purple fall back to "congratulations").
5. System publishes `player_analytics` for each player (when `analytics.enabled` is on; UC-041 BR-006) and `game_ended` with `winning_team` (the team index, `none` when no team won), `outcome` and, after a force-end, `reason` (BR-004, UC-020 BR-007).

## Alternative Flows

### A1: Random Teams formation

**Trigger:** Mode is JoustRandomTeams (step 1)
**Flow:**

1. System shuffles the team colours, shuffles the players (sorted by serial) and assigns them round-robin; team sizes differ by at most one; with fewer players than teams the extra teams stay empty.
2. System opens the gameplay stream, then runs a 5 s `team_formation` phase (`team_formation_start` / `team_formation_end`) so players find their team: each controller pulses its team colour over the gameplay stream, then keeps it as its base colour. Frames that arrive during the formation and the instructions clip are discarded. A disconnect seen in that time is handled when the game loop starts (UC-020 A2), unless the controller's frames came back before the formation and the instructions ended (UC-002 BR-004); a drop and reconnect during the countdown is still eliminated. A stream that breaks or stalls in that time is opened once more when the instructions end.
3. Use case continues at step 2.

### A2: All teams eliminated together

**Trigger:** No team has players alive (step 4)
**Flow:**

1. System publishes `game_tie`.
2. Use case continues at step 5.

### A3: Instructions on

**Trigger:** The host has switched spoken instructions on (`game_instructions`, FR-014, the pad's K6, UC-015 BR-011) when the game starts (step 2)
**Flow:**

1. System runs the base instructions flow (UC-020 A5) with the `Teams-instructions` clip (both Teams and Random Teams). Random Teams has a streamed intro: it plays the clip after the `team_formation` phase, while the stream is drained (A1 step 2).
2. Use case continues at step 2.

## Postconditions

### Success Postconditions

- The winning team is in the event stream; the session is ENDED.

### Failure Postconditions

- `game_error` ends the session.

## Business Rules

### BR-001: Team colours

Teams uses the first N of 8 team colours; Random Teams uses a random N of the 8.

### BR-002: Root-span outcome - Obsolete

Obsolete. The root-span outcome is the base rule UC-020 BR-006; the Team Joust values are in BR-004. The ID is kept so it is not reused.

### BR-003: Force-end declares no winner; the win is latched - Obsolete

Obsolete. The force-end and win-latch rule is the base rule UC-020 BR-007; the Team Joust values are in BR-004. The ID is kept so it is not reused.

### BR-004: Delta from the base lifecycle

| Row | Teams, Random Teams |
|---|---|
| Win condition | only one team has players alive (step 4); none alive: `game_tie` (A2) |
| Teams | `num_teams` 2 to 6 (default 2), round-robin by serial (Random Teams: shuffled players and colours, A1); colours per BR-001 |
| Thresholds | the tempo-driven thresholds, default kill gate (UC-030) |
| Start cues | team countdown (team colour, white, green); Random Teams: the 5 s `team_formation` pulse first (A1) |
| Result event and end cues | `team_winner` (team, name, colour, winners) + rainbow on the winners + "<colour> team wins" (Pink, Orange, Purple: "congratulations"); `game_tie` (A2) |
| No-winner value in `game_ended` | `winning_team` `none` |
| Instruction clip (A3) | `Teams-instructions` |
| Streamed intro | Random Teams only (A1) |
| `run()` | base |
| Mode grace | none |
| Eliminations | every death |

Force-end and latch (UC-020 BR-007): no `team_winner`, no rainbow, no team victory voice; the surviving player and team spans end with `winner=false`. Outcome (UC-020 BR-006): `completed` or `force_ended`.
